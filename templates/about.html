What is AyuSetu?

AyuSetu is an online healthcare appointment platform designed to make finding and consulting healthcare professionals easier and more convenient.

The name AyuSetu comes from two meaningful words: Ayu, representing life and health, and Setu, meaning bridge.

Our goal is to create a trusted bridge between patients and qualified healthcare professionals.
